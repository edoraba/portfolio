I build products in React, Next.js and Astro, from marketing sites to bespoke back office systems: the interface, the API, the database and the deploy. As a partner at Redergo I coordinate the developers on a ten-person team.

I came to it from design. I started at Redergo in 2020 designing interfaces in Figma and kept opening the code to fix what the handoff lost, and in 2021 I stopped handing off. The training is still what I reach for when a product needs a decision rather than a component, but the work is development.

Before design school I spent two years in energy engineering at Politecnico di Torino. It did not become a degree, but it is where I learned to think in systems and constraints. The degree came from IAAD, in Digital Communication Design.

## How I work

I care about the parts that usually get skipped: empty states, loading states, error copy. I keep pull requests small and attach a screenshot when the change is visual. When a decision is not obvious, I write down the trade-off instead of leaving it in my head. Specs come before code; I run Claude Code as a fixed part of my workflow, driven by structured Markdown specs, and brought the practice into the team.
