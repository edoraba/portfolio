I started at Redergo in 2020 designing interfaces in Figma and kept opening the code to fix what the handoff lost. In 2021 I stopped handing off. Today I build products in React, Next.js and Astro, from marketing sites to bespoke back office systems, and as a partner I coordinate the developers on a ten-person team.

Before design school I spent two years in energy engineering at Politecnico di Torino. It did not become a degree, but it is where I learned to think in systems and constraints. The degree came from IAAD, in Digital Communication Design.

## How I work

Coming from design, I care about the parts that usually get skipped: empty states, loading states, error copy. I keep pull requests small and attach a screenshot when the change is visual. When a decision is not obvious, I write down the trade-off instead of leaving it in my head. Specs come before code; I run Claude Code as a fixed part of my workflow, driven by structured Markdown specs, and brought the practice into the team.
