# Plan 05: The home plates

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans (inline, one task at a time, commit per task, visual review per task). Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Turn the home into the seven-plate story of spec section 6, each plate a `.sheet` section with its own scroll choreography, in the spirit Edoardo asked for ("layout speciali tutti in movimento allo scroll, racconta una storia", wodniack.dev).

**Architecture:** Every plate is a client component under `components/plates/`, registered with `usePlate(id, ref)` so the console readout follows it. Choreography uses GSAP ScrollTrigger (`scrub: true`, pins at most 200vh desktop and 100vh mobile, one pin on screen at a time) created inside `useGSAP` with a scope so it reverts on unmount. Transforms and clip-path only; nothing scrubs filters. Every plate has three states: desktop full motion, mobile (below 768px, no long pins, simplified), reduced motion (static layout, 150ms fades). All copy is real DOM text; decorative letter grids are `aria-hidden`.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16, GSAP 3.15 ScrollTrigger on the Tempus clock (`setupGsap`), Lenis (already on the home), CSS 3D perspective (no second WebGL), Content Collections data.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-04-v2-ruled-sheet-design.md` section 6 (plates), 8 (hover), 9 (motion rules), 11 (gates).
