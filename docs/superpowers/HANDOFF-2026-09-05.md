## 2. What changed on 2026-09-05

In the order it happened, with the reason, because most of these were defects he found rather
than features anyone planned.

**The field got an owner and a shape.** Claims already decided who holds the one WebGL surface;
a claim can now also name an SVG mask, so the canvas is cut to whatever asked for it instead of
only to the headline. Four things claim it: the headline and its strip, the mark of P/02, the lab
plate of P/04, the strip above the footer. Anything that can show it also prints the same dither
when it does not have it, because the field has one owner and a second claimant would otherwise
go blank.

**The hero strip stopped flickering** without going back to a printed pattern. The shader samples
its noise at an offset taken from the scroll and quantised to whole cells, so the pattern travels
with the page, and the flow clock holds still while the page moves.

**Borders, everywhere.** Per-cell edges break at every gutter and double up across one. The model
is now: horizontal hairlines are continuous `Rule` elements, vertical ones are a cell's left line,
and only the last cell of a band closes it. `scripts/borders.mjs` reads the content lines off the
grid and reports any band that opens or closes off it, or any pair drawing two rules a gutter
apart. It found ten bands whose right edge fell away as the sheet reflowed; `rowEdge()` in
`components/sheet/edge.ts` closes those at the widths where the cell is actually last. The same
audit is an e2e test over eight routes.

**Type.** Bricolage is gone. Funnel Display carries the big type through `next/font/google`;
Switzer reads the text, self-hosted from files fetched at build time because its licence permits
self-hosting but not publishing the files in a public repo (`assets/fonts/README.md`). Funnel has
no italic and no width axis, so the headline animates weight instead, and the emphasised word
carries extra weight rather than leaning. Display sizes were re-measured at eight widths from 320
to 1920.

**P/02 about** was a flat drawer holding a 96px mark with an animation that never ran. It is now
the mark at the scale of an object, its ten bars cut out of the live field, drawing themselves in
stems first as the plate arrives. Its columns were crossing: the facts ran to column nine while
the drawer started at eight.

**P/04 notes** held a dither so faint it read as an empty box. It holds a window onto the field
instead, which is the piece the list points at.

**P/06 since** flew five bordered cards with an empty panel where an image would go. There are no
images, so the moments are ruled bands now, arriving one at a time out of the vanishing point.

**P/03 work** was rebuilt three times against `wodniack.dev`, which Edoardo asked for by name. It
ended as: a hole cut in the sheet showing a fixed layer, so coming down the page is a parallax
through a window; the octagon zooms keeping its shape until its corners are off the screen and the
void is everything, with ruled squares outside it scaling at the same rate; the word is really
eight rows of thirteen copies stacked in one place, which part onto a shell around the reader that
keeps turning; the projects cross it scattered. On the way out it all closes back and the plate is
a plate again.

**The console** lights a whole switch: the hover ran to the cell edge and left the 24px gutter
dark, which read as half a button lighting up. It now runs to the next cell's line, and the last
cell in a row stops at its own.

**Footer dither** went from 96px to 220px at full strength; its top fade was a fixed slice of the
viewport whatever the strip's height, which washed out a short one completely.

## 4. Gotchas this session added

All of them are written up where they belong, in `docs/motion.md`. The four that cost the most
time:

- A pinned element carries a transform, and a transformed ancestor is the containing block for
  everything `position: fixed` inside it. A fixed layer in a pinned plate is positioned against
  the plate, not the viewport.
- A clip path clips its whole subtree. Any control the reader must reach goes outside the clipped
  element.
- `Plate` owns rows 1 and 2, so a plate whose content names rows starts at 3. Grid auto placement
  does not go backwards: a sheet that claims row 2 leaves its own header nowhere to go but the
  bottom.
- Depth is worth solving from apparent size rather than easing on its own, or a thing coming
  towards the reader crawls while it is far and leaps past exactly when it needs to be read.

## 5. Working notes

- Do not wait on CI for small changes; verify locally and push. The full local set is in the
  README's Verify section, and `scripts/borders.mjs` and `GPU=1 scripts/shots.mjs` are the two
  that catch what tests do not.
- Headless Chromium has no hardware WebGL, so the field silently falls back and every field test
  passes with the field broken. Use `GPU=1`, which opens a headed browser off-screen.
- Kill anything on port 3100 before `pnpm test:e2e`, or Playwright reuses a stale server and you
  will chase a ghost.
- Look at what you built before reporting it. Most of this session's list came from Edoardo
  looking at the screen, not from a test failing.
